Change or cancel a booking

  • Offers a better option before any cancel
  • Saves notes like an allergy, with a reminder to tell the host

Answer restaurant questions

  • Hours, menu and parking from facts on file
  • Policies from a Data 360 library, with citations
  • Says “not on file” and offers a person

Hand off to a person

  • A Case with an AI summary, so no one re-asks

  • After hours: a Case number and a follow-up
  • Triggered by a request, frustration or a sensitive issue
